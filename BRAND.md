# Atulyash: what the brand is

Facts, audience, voice and rules. There is deliberately nothing here about how the website should look: no fonts, colours, layouts or past designs. The look is yours to invent.

## The company and the product

- Atulyash Foods Private Limited. Website atulyash.com, Instagram @atulyashfoods.
- One product: fresh whole wheat atta, ₹120 a kilo. Sold in packs from 2 kg; buy once, or set a weekly plan that can be skipped or paused.
- Single-origin MP Sharbati wheat, cleaned in several stages, milled slowly on natural stone chakkis in small batches. The chokar (bran) is kept in, nothing is added.
- Milled the day before delivery and brought by Atulyash's own riders, to select South and North Delhi pincodes.
- Lines the brand already uses: "Atta in its purest form" (on the pack), "Freshly milled. Delivered weekly." (brand line), "Incomparable Purity. No Compromise." (under the logo), #AttaDeservesRespect.
- Expert: Dr. Asha Kawatra, 35 years in foods and nutrition, formerly Head of Foods and Nutrition at CCS HAU, Hisar. She appears in six short films and one longer film (links in PAGE.md). Quote her only with her exact words and their context.
- An app is coming on Android and iOS. Don't show store badges until it is live.

What the pack itself says, word for word:

> Made from 100% MP Sharbati Wheat: Never Mixed. Never Blended.
> Slow Natural Stone Milled: No Industrial Steel Rollers. No Artificial Emery Stones. Ground at Controlled, Low Speed.
> Full Fibre, The Soul of Atta: Bran Intact. Nothing Removed.
> Freshly Milled in Small Batches: Delivered Soon After Grinding. Not Produced for Long-Term Storage.
> No exaggerated health claims. Just atta in its purest form.

## Who it is for

Older Delhi NCR households who care about family food. Adult children buying for their parents may be a second group (not yet tested). Every line must make sense to a 60-year-old on first read: plain words, large readable text, nothing that needs explaining.

## The idea behind the brand

Roti is on the plate every day in most North Indian homes, but the atta behind it is bought on autopilot: the same bag, the same shop, never questioned. Atulyash asks families to give that habit a second look, without ever saying their current atta is bad. The honest edge is that Atulyash shows every choice openly: the wheat, the stone, the chokar, the milling day.

The manifesto, close to the founder's own words:

> We keep changing everything around us. Our skincare, our cooking oil, even our utensils, because we research what is best for our family. So why don't we stop and think about our atta? Its quality, where it comes from, how it is made. We just take their word for it.

Six questions anyone can ask of any packet: Which wheat? How was it cleaned? Natural stone, emery or roller mill? How fast was it milled? Is the chokar kept in? When was it milled?

## Voice

Plain, short and warm. Questions more than statements. English for explanations, Hinglish or Hindi for family moments and for Veer. No jargon, no hype words, no "revolutionary".

## Veer, the mascot

Veer is the village boy on every Atulyash pack, and the face of the brand (think of the Amul girl). The supplied drawings are in `assets/veer/`.

- **Who he is:** a village boy who knows wheat from the field. Curious, a little mischievous, always playful. He asks questions; that is his whole job.
- **How he talks:** one short Hinglish question at a time. His signature line: "Isse kisi ne poocha?" (Did anyone ask about this one?). Others: "Toh asli farak kahaan padta hai?" (So where does the real difference lie?), "Veer ne poocha. Aapne kabhi poocha?" (Veer asked. Have you ever asked?).
- **Looks:** yellow kurta, green dhoti-style pants, a green headband with a patterned tail.
- **He may be restyled to fit the design you choose** (drawing style, palette, texture). Regenerating him with AI image tools is allowed, using the supplied drawings as the reference, as long as he stays clearly the same boy: same face, headband, village clothes.
- **Never:** make him a salesman (no prices, offers or "buy now" in his mouth), give him claims or comparisons, dress him as a city kid, show him mocking anyone, or compare him to a religious figure.

## Fixed artwork

- **Logo** (`assets/logo/`): the serif wordmark ATULYASH with अतुल्यश tucked into the H, a green-and-gold leaf "A" mark, and the line "Incomparable Purity. No Compromise." Don't redraw it. A single-colour version (all dark or all white) to suit your background is fine.
- **Pack** (`assets/pack/`): the white 2 kg pouch with the village scene and Veer. Show it as it is.

## Rules that apply to every line and image

- Never say or imply that another atta is bad, unsafe, refined, chemically treated or "stripped", or that Atulyash is the only stone-milled or Sharbati atta. Other brands sell those too.
- No health results and no taste promises.
- Never say "our farm".
- Price comparisons only with real, current, checked numbers.
- The brand's illustrations in `assets/illustrations/` are AI-made: use them as illustrations, never as photos of Atulyash's real mill, wheat or customers.
- Don't put "Concept image", "Concept art" or any similar label on the page.
- No video shoots or filming in any proposal.
- Writing: never use the long em dash. No footer text of any kind (no source lines, disclaimers or credits parked along the bottom).
