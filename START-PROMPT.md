You are designing the homepage of Atulyash from a blank page. Atulyash makes fresh, stone-milled whole wheat atta and delivers it weekly to homes in Delhi.

**Start clean.** Work only from this folder (`C:\Users\Asus\Desktop\Content Strategy\Atulyash\Atulyash-fresh`). Don't open, read, search or reuse any file or folder outside it unless I ask you to in this chat. That includes the rest of the parent `Atulyash` folder (its website repo, plans, exports, inspiration board and FOLDERMAP), the content-brain folder, the GitHub staging site, atulyash.com's current look, and any earlier Atulyash website, pitch or design system. If you think something outside this folder would help, ask me first. If you remember earlier Atulyash designs, fonts or colours, set them aside: they are exactly what this session is replacing. No assumed fonts, palette, layout or motif carries over.

**Read first:**
1. `BRAND.md`: who Atulyash is, the audience, the voice, Veer, and the rules every line and image must follow.
2. `COPY.md`: the words to use, as written.
3. `PAGE.md`: what each homepage section has to do, plus links and the calculator logic.
4. `assets/`: the logo, the pack, Veer's supplied drawings, Dr. Asha's photo and the brand's own illustrations.
5. `inspiration/` and `inspiration/STYLES.md`: my inspiration, sorted into six styles. **These are the only styles you may use.** Every direction must come from one of them; don't invent other styles or bring in ones from elsewhere.

**The task:** design the opening of the homepage, meaning the first three scrolls (Hero, Manifesto, The Atulyash way), in **five completely different directions, each built from one of my inspiration styles**. There are six styles, so in step 1 propose one direction per style and I'll pick the five to build. Each direction has its own:
- type pairing,
- colour palette,
- composition and layout idea (not the same skeleton reskinned),
- way of using imagery,
- Veer, restyled to belong in that direction (AI regeneration from the supplied drawings is allowed; he must stay recognisably the same boy, labelled "Concept art").

They must also differ clearly from one another. It's for older Delhi families, so text must be large and easy to read, and every page must work beautifully on a phone first (390 px wide) and then on desktop (1280 px).

**How to work:**
1. Before building anything, show me one direction per inspiration style (six) in short: which style it comes from, a name, the mood in one line, the fonts, the palette, the big layout idea and how Veer will look. Wait for me to pick five.
2. Then build each as a static HTML page in `opening/1-…` to `opening/5-…`, plus `opening/index.html` linking them.
3. Check each page at 390 px and 1280 px: no sideways scrolling, no console errors, text contrast at least WCAG AA, tap targets at least 44 px, and every AI image or art labelled. Send me screenshots of all five.
4. Ask me before publishing anything anywhere.

**Always:** plain words, no em dashes, no footer text, no health or taste claims, no comparisons with other brands, never "the only" or "our farm", and no filming or video shoots in any idea.
