What goes into every pack

  1. The wheat

    Single-origin MP Sharbati. Never blended with other varieties.

  2. The cleaning

    In several stages.

  3. The stone

    Natural stone chakkis. No emery stone, no steel rollers.

  4. The chokar

    Chokar kept in. Nothing removed, nothing added.

  5. The milling day

    Milled in small batches the day before, delivered the next morning.

  6. The delivery

    We deliver with our own riders. Packed with minimal handling.
