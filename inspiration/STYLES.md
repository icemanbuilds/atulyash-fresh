# The owner's inspiration styles

These pictures are the only visual starting points for the design. Every direction you propose comes from one of these styles, so don't bring in styles of your own. Take each style's look (palette idea, imagery, type feel, layout devices) and make it fit Atulyash. Copy no one's logo, words or artwork.

Some pictures contain things that break Atulyash's rules: made-up numbers ("500M+", "25,000+ Acres", "15+ Years"), "100% Organic" or "AI-powered" badges, star ratings, discount countdowns and partner-logo rows. Borrow the look, never those.
