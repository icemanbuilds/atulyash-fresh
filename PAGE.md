# What the homepage has to do

The sections of the homepage and the job each one does, plus where things link. This is about content and purpose only: the order, shape and look of every section are yours to decide. The words are in COPY.md.

| Section | Its job |
| --- | --- |
| Hero | Say what Atulyash is in one glance, and give two ways forward: see packs and prices, or check delivery to my area. |
| Manifesto | Make the reader pause on a habit: we question everything else, why not our atta? Veer asks "Isse kisi ne poocha?" |
| The Atulyash way | Show the choices behind the atta: the wheat, the cleaning, the stone, the chokar, the milling day, the riders. |
| Six questions for any packet | Let readers check what their own packet tells them (tick each question it answers, see a count out of 6), and see how Atulyash answers each. |
| Dr. Asha films | Let readers pick a short film by topic and watch it on YouTube. |
| Honest price and roti calculator | Show the price plainly (₹120 a kg, about ₹3.60 a roti) and help a family work out how much atta they need each week from their rotis a day. |
| Weekly plan | Choose every week or just once, choose the quantity, see the order and the price, add to cart. |
| Close | One last way in: check delivery by PIN code, call or WhatsApp, or start an order. |

**The opening** means the first three: Hero, Manifesto, The Atulyash way.

## Calculator logic

Rotis on a usual day (1 to 60, starting at 8) × 30 g × 7 days, plus extra for parathas and pooris: rarely +15% (the default), once a week +20%, a few times a week +25%, most days +30%. Show kg a week to one decimal and about how much over 4 weeks. Suggest the smallest of 2, 4, 6 or 8 kg a week that covers it; above 8 kg, offer a call. Price is ₹120 a kg (2 kg ₹240, 4 kg ₹480, 6 kg ₹720, 8 kg ₹960).
